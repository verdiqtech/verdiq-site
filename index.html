Products

Verdiq Book

An SMS- and voice-first scheduling platform for solo service providers. Clients book by texting or calling the provider's dedicated Verdiq number — no apps to install, no web forms to fill out. The platform handles the conversation, surfaces requests for the provider to confirm with a single SMS reply, and runs the rest of the booking lifecycle automatically.

Features include booking confirmations, automated reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before each appointment, reschedule and cancellation notices, recurring bookings, vacation cascade with auto-reschedule, and a public booking page each provider can share on their bio, business card, or QR code.

Book by text — SMS program

Verdiq Book's messaging program is the heart of the product. This section describes what we send, how clients opt in and out, and where the program is governed.

What we send

The program is strictly transactional and conversational. We do not send marketing, promotional, or third-party content through this program.

  • Booking confirmations when a client requests a service.
  • Appointment reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before each booking.
  • Reschedule and cancellation notices when the provider or client modifies an appointment, including provider-initiated vacation cascades with a magic link to pick a new time.
  • Two-way conversational replies that let clients confirm, reschedule, ask questions about their bookings, or stop messaging.

How clients opt in

Clients consent to SMS communication at the point of booking, in one of three ways: by texting the provider's published Verdiq number to initiate a conversation; by submitting their phone number on the provider's public booking page (which displays the consent disclosure above the call-to-action); or by replying YES to a one-time consent prompt when the provider enters the number on the client's behalf.

Consent disclosure

By texting the published booking number or submitting your number on a provider's Verdiq booking page, you agree to receive booking-related SMS from the provider via Verdiq Book. Message frequency is typically 3–6 messages per booking (confirmation, reminders, and any reschedule or cancellation notices). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, QUIT, or CANCEL at any time to opt out of future messages. Reply HELP or INFO for help. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.
